Notes on the data

  • Documented political giving is the larger of two figures: the sum of 527 giving 2025-26, 527 giving 2021-24 and state PAC giving, or the New England list's all-years totals. The NE totals already span every year, so adding them on top would double count. Every raw field is in the record panel.
  • Largest published gift band is the floor of the band an institution publishes (for example "$100,000 and up"). It shows up in records but isn't in the political giving total. One record (a band floor of $2) looks like a data-entry slip.
  • 1,792 records carry no state, mostly published donor rolls that list names only. They drop out of the map but stay in every other view.
  • De-duplication across lists matched on name and, where available, state. 36 records carry a "Possible matches to check" note. Those are shown in the record panel and are worth a look before outreach.
  • Source: Rosc Master Conservative Donor Prospect List, Master tab (2,828 rows, 29 columns). The New England and On 2+ lists tabs are filtered copies of Master, so both are reachable here through the filters above.
